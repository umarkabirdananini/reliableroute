Which ports do you cover?

Our coverage includes Tin Can and Lekki in Lagos, Rivers Port in Port Harcourt, Onne in Rivers State, Calabar, and Delta Port Complex in Delta State. We also support PTML and Five Star Logistics Terminal operations in Lagos.

Can you arrange delivery outside Lagos?

Yes. Our operational coverage extends across Nigeria, with haulage and transport coordination from port to destination. Tell us the delivery location and cargo details when you enquire.

What do you need to prepare a quote?

Start with the service you need, your cargo or vehicle details, origin, destination and preferred timing. Our team can then clarify the documents and shipment information needed for your requirements.

Do you work with international companies?

We welcome partnerships with shipping, automobile, trading and logistics businesses that need customs clearance, cargo handling, transportation and local delivery support in Nigeria.

Can you help with vehicle imports?

Our auto logistics service includes import and export support for vehicles, handling and documentation. Share the vehicle details and destination to start the conversation.
